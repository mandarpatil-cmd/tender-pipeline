Scrape portal

The portal search is AOC, Award of Contract. That is the awarded list.

Each page and each tender waits at least this long. The shortest pause is 1.5 seconds.

Contract dates fill fromDate and toDate. Published dates fill publishedFromDate and publishedToDate. Leave a pair empty to leave that portal field blank.

Starts with one tender. If the automatic reader fails, the image appears on the run page.
